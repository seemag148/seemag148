# Book-to-Business Authority Graph

## The Problem

Most AI author tools focus on writing, editing, formatting, or publishing the book.

For an expert, entrepreneur, consultant, or speaker, the larger business question comes after the manuscript:

How does this intellectual property create authority, opportunity, and revenue?

## Product Idea

Book-to-Business Authority Graph analyzes a manuscript, book, framework, or body of expertise and maps it to business opportunities.

### Inputs

- manuscript or published book
- author bio
- current offers
- target audience
- speaking history
- business goals

### Outputs

- Authority Pillars
- Signature Ideas
- Speaking Topic Map
- Corporate Workshop Opportunities
- Podcast and Media Angles
- Partnership Opportunities
- Lead Magnet Ideas
- Consulting Offer Extensions
- Content Series
- AI and search discoverability opportunities
- 90-day book-to-business activation plan

## Differentiator

This is not an AI book writer.

It is an IP commercialization engine.

The product starts where most author tools stop.
