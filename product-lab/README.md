# Seema Giri AI Product Lab

This is a public working lab for practical AI products at the intersection of human judgment, authority, leadership, and execution.

The goal is not to create more generic prompt packs.

The goal is to build tools that help people make better decisions, turn expertise into opportunity, and use AI without losing the human value that creates trust.

## Current Product Tracks

### 1. Decision-to-Delivery OS
A project and leadership intelligence system that turns scattered project information into the decisions, alignment, risks, and next actions that actually move work forward.

### 2. Book-to-Business Authority Graph
A commercialization and authority system that turns a book or body of expertise into speaking, consulting, training, partnerships, content, and discoverability opportunities.

### 3. Human Advantage Map
An AI-era career and workforce system that identifies what to automate, what to augment, and where human judgment becomes more valuable.

## Product Principles

- Solve an expensive business problem, not an interesting AI problem
- Use AI to improve judgment, not just generate more output
- Create evidence and next actions, not generic advice
- Keep a human decision point where risk, reputation, money, or people are involved
- Design for real business workflows
- Build small prototypes, test them with real users, then expand
