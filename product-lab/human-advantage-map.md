# Human Advantage Map

## The Problem

Professionals and organizations are asking the wrong AI question:

"What can AI do?"

The more useful question is:

"Where should AI take over, where should it assist, and where does human judgment become more valuable?"

## Product Idea

Human Advantage Map evaluates a role, team, or workflow and divides work into three categories.

### Automate
Repeatable tasks where AI can reduce manual effort.

### Augment
Work where AI improves speed, analysis, or preparation but a human remains accountable.

### Elevate
Work where judgment, trust, influence, context, relationships, leadership, and lived experience create disproportionate value.

## Outputs

- Automate / Augment / Elevate Map
- Human Advantage Score
- Role Risk and Opportunity Map
- Skills to strengthen
- AI skills to acquire
- Work to stop doing
- 30 / 60 / 90-day transition plan
- Proof-of-work project recommendations
- Manager or team conversation guide

## Differentiator

Most AI readiness tools measure technology adoption.

Human Advantage Map measures how work should change while protecting and increasing human value.
